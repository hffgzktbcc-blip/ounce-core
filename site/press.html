Ounce-Core press & research kit

For technical writers, researchers and prospective testers seeking accurate, dated descriptions of Ounce-Core. Please use the specific CI run and commit being described rather than an uncited headline number.

Short description

Ounce-Core is an open-source Rust AArch64 research runtime for permitted homebrew workloads, paired with Ounce-Verify tools to record and check execution evidence. Its architecture explores interpreters, tiered execution, memory, services and graphics. It is not a commercial-game product or a general-purpose Switch 2 compatibility claim.

What can be claimed today?

  • The project maintains a growing suite of externally authored, pinned original-Switch homebrew workloads with first-frame evidence evaluated separately for interpreter and Tier-3 execution. Check the latest CI artifacts for the exact current pass count; 50 registered inputs is not equivalent to 50 passing inputs or 50 independent users.
  • Some GPU-host, native Mac, and experimental Switch 2 software-path work is being developed and tested with separate scope restrictions. See GPU qualification and the project plan.
  • External copies, questions and issue reports are public indicators of attention; they do not establish independent successful execution.

What is not proven?

Do not interpret a build, a synthetic harness pass, a rendered host window, or a fork as proof of broad guest compatibility. General Switch 2 software compatibility, retail games, commercial products and independently reproduced broad coverage are not claimed.
