== Timestamp ordering caveat ==
The newlib (built 17:37:18, recipe committed 17:40:01), switch-examples
(built 23:27:35, committed 23:29:17) and libnx (built 2026-02-04 13:39,
4.12.0 recipe committed 2026-02-05 07:28) packages were each built minutes
to a day BEFORE their matching public recipe commits. This is consistent
with a build-then-push-release workflow, but the commits above are the
NEAREST public recipes, not proven build inputs. A reviewer should treat the
signed binary packages as the primary artifacts and these recipes as their
best public explanation.
